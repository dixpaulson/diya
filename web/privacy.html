Privacy Policy

Diya · Last updated 28 October 2026

In short: Diya does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

What the app saves on your device

To remember your choices between visits, Diya saves the following on your phone or, on the website, in your browser's storage:

  • your chosen deity, and the prayers and verses you save
  • your city, used to work out Panchang timings
  • your language
  • your date, time, and place of birth, only if you choose to enter them
  • your daily streak, the date you last opened the app, and whether you have finished the welcome screens

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any information.
  • The phone app works offline. On Android, it does not even have permission to use the internet.

Sharing

When you tap Share, for example on a verse card or the day's Panchang, Diya opens your device's share menu with the text or picture you chose. What happens after that is up to you and the app you share it to.

The website

The web version of Diya is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The website also downloads parts of the app, such as its display engine and some fonts, from Google's servers (gstatic.com), so Google may also see your IP address.

Removing your information

Because everything is stored on your device, you are in control of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it, or you can clear the app's storage in your phone's settings. On the website, clear this site's data in your browser.

Children

Diya does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
